### 2. What data we process

**If you sign up as a resident**
- Your email address, to sign in with a code.
- Your postal code, which tells us which town you belong to, and your language.
- If you add them: first and last name, phone number and date of birth.
- What you do in the programme: points earned and spent, shops whose code you scanned and
  when, rewards redeemed and their voucher code, and the invitations you sent or signed up
  with.
- The dates you signed up, of each movement and of when you accepted the terms.

**If you register a shop**
- Business details: name, tax ID, address, postal code, phone, website, opening hours,
  categories, description, logo and photos.
- Contact email and, if you give it, the name of the person who runs it.
- Scans received and vouchers validated.

**If you are the contact person of a town council or an association**
- The organisation's name and the role, email, phone or address it publishes on its website or in the
  municipal directory. We only use them to propose the programme.

**If you write to us through the website form**
- Name, role, organisation, town, email and whatever you tell us.

**If you book a meeting on the website**
- Name, email and whatever you write in the booking calendar (Calendly).

**Technical data**
- Our servers keep the IP address and time of each request for a short period, so the
  service works and abuse can be detected.

We do not ask for health data, beliefs or any other specially protected data, nor for bank
details.

### 4. How long we keep it

- **Your account and activity:** while you have an account. The pilot lasts 60 days, followed
  by 30 more days to redeem points. If the programme does not continue, we delete or anonymise
  the accounts **3 months after the pilot ends**. If it continues, we will tell you first and
  you can leave.
- **If you ask to leave:** we delete your data or make it anonymous (points movements and
  redemptions remain only as figures, with nothing that identifies you), within one month at
  most.
- **Sign-in codes:** expire after 10 minutes. We only keep a fingerprint of the code, never
  the code itself.
- **Website form:** up to 24 months after the last contact.
- **Technical logs of the servers and email delivery:** as set by each provider, usually days
  or weeks (email, 30 days).
- If a law requires us to keep something longer (for example, for a claim), we keep it
  blocked and unused, only for that time (Spanish Data Protection Act, Art. 32).

### 6. What we store on your phone or browser

Only what the App needs to work: your session, language, postal code and town, and, if you
arrive through an invitation, its code for 30 days so the person who invited you gets their
points. None of this is used for advertising.

To know how many visits the App gets, we count each visit with the town and the day. We store
nothing on your device to do this, and no data that identifies you.

The km0lab.com website only stores your language. The YouTube video and the Calendly
calendar are explained in its cookie policy.

### 7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:
- **See** what data we hold about you and get a copy.
- **Correct** anything that is wrong (you can change most of it yourself in your profile).
- **Delete** your account and your data.
- **Object** to a particular use or ask us to **restrict** it.
- **Take it with you** in a machine-readable format.
- **Withdraw your consent** to share your phone, by turning it off in your profile.

**How:** write to **info@km0lab.com** from the email you signed up with, saying what you want.
It is free. We reply within one month at most (GDPR, Art. 12(3)).

If you think we have not respected your rights, you can complain to the **Spanish Data
Protection Agency** (www.aepd.es).

### 8. Minimum age

You must be **18 or older** to take part in the pilot. If we learn that a minor has signed up,
we delete the account.

### 9. Security

You sign in with a one-time code that expires after 10 minutes, without a password.
Connections are encrypted. Only the people who need your data for the programme can see it.
If a security incident put your data at risk, we would report it to the authority within 72
hours (GDPR, Art. 33) and, if the risk were high, to you too.

### 10. Changes

If we change this policy we will tell you in the App before it applies. The date at the top
