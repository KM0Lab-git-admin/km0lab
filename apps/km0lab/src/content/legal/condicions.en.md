KM0 LAB (the name under which Albert Malleu Poch operates,
Calle del Mar 65, local bajo, 08380 Malgrat de Mar, info@km0lab.com) runs a pilot programme in your town to reward
visits to local shops, supported by a shopkeepers' association or the town council. These
terms explain how it works. By signing up, you accept them.

### 1. The pilot

- It starts once at least **10 shops** in the town have joined and lasts **60 days**. The
  start and end dates are shown in the App. It may stop earlier in the cases in section 4.
- It is a trial for a small group of residents. There may be errors, and we may change things
  while it runs. If a change affects you (for example, points or rewards), we will tell you in
  the App before it applies.
- Taking part is free and does not require you to buy anything.
- You must be **14 or older**. One account per person, whichever way you sign in.
- Each pilot accepts up to **200 accounts**, in order of sign-up. Once it is full, no more are
  accepted.

### 2. What points are

- Points are a way of thanking you for taking part. **They are not money**: they cannot be
  exchanged for money, sold or transferred to someone else.
- When you sign up you get **50 welcome points**.
- When you visit a participating shop and scan its code, you get **100 points**. Each shop gives
  points **only once** per person for the whole pilot.
- For each person you invite you get **50 points**, once that person completes sign-up. There
  is a maximum number of friends who earn you points, which depends on how you joined the
  pilot (it may be zero). The App shows it to you.

### 3. Rewards

- Rewards are provided by **KM0 LAB**. You redeem them in the App with the points each one
  requires.
- Each reward requires **100 points for every euro** of its value.
- **Subject to availability.** When a reward runs out, it is no longer shown. There is no
  obligation to restock it.
- When you redeem a reward you get a **voucher with a code**. Show it at the shop indicated
  and the shop validates it. A voucher can only be used once.
- A credit voucher gives no cash change if you spend less than its value.

### 4. Until when

- When the pilot ends you have **30 more days** to redeem your points and use your vouchers.
  After that, points expire and vouchers are no longer valid.
- If the programme continues, we will tell you beforehand and explain what happens to your
  points.

**If we stop the pilot early**

- KM0 LAB may **stop the pilot in a town**, for a few days or for good, before the 60 days are
  up, only for one of these reasons: the rewards have run out, the budget KM0 LAB sets aside
  for the pilot has run out, there is abuse that cannot be stopped any other way, or a
  technical or legal problem prevents it from continuing.
- We will tell you in the App and by email **7 days in advance**. If all rewards have run out
  or the reason is beyond our control, we will tell you at that moment.
- From the notice, no new points can be earned.
- You can redeem the points you already have for **30 days** from the notice, while rewards
  last. Vouchers you already hold are valid until the end of that period.
- If the stop lasts only a few days, you keep your points and carry on when it resumes.
- If no rewards are left, any points you have not redeemed expire without compensation:
  they are not money.
- You can also leave the pilot whenever you want (section 6).

### 5. Fair play

We may **cancel points, vouchers or the account**, after telling you and hearing what you
have to say, if we see that you:
- have more than one account or use other people's or made-up email addresses or social
  network accounts;
- scan a shop's code without having been there (for example, from a photo);
- invite yourself with another account;
- exploit App errors to earn points.

If an App error gives you points you should not have, we may correct them.

### 6. Your account

- You sign in with your email and a code we send you, or with Google, Facebook or Apple.
  Keep access to your email and those accounts safe.
- You can leave at any time by writing to info@km0lab.com. If you leave, you lose any points
  you have not redeemed.
- Your data is processed as explained in the privacy policy.

### 7. Liability

KM0 LAB tries to keep the App working well, but during the pilot there may be outages or
errors. If you lose points you earned fairly because of our mistake, we will give them back.
Each shop is responsible for what it sells and how it serves you.

### 8. Applicable law

Spanish law applies. If you are a consumer, you may bring a claim before the courts of your
place of residence. For any question or complaint: info@km0lab.com.

### For shops {#comercos}

If you register a shop, in addition:
- You must be an adult and the shop's owner, or have the owner's permission.
- The listing details (name, address, phone, website, opening hours, description and photos)
  are published in the App. The contact email and tax ID are not.
- You undertake that the information is true and to display the shop's code at the counter,
  and only there.
- You may not scan your own code or validate your own or your family's vouchers.
- You validate the vouchers shown to you in the App, while they are valid.
- If a minor shows you a voucher, you may not redeem it for alcohol or tobacco.
- KM0 LAB pays you the amount of the credit vouchers you validate, in the way agreed when you
  join.
- You may leave the pilot at any time by telling us. Your listing will no longer be shown.
